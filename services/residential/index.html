Residential architecture

We design bespoke homes across Aotearoa New Zealand. We start with your household and the site, then work through the floor plan, the light and the materials together.

A brief grounded in everyday life

We ask how a typical day works in your home. Where coats and bags land, how the kitchen is used and where you go for quiet all affect the plan. Those conversations help us agree on what the design needs to do.

Make the most of the site

We test where rooms and outdoor spaces could sit to make use of daylight, views and shelter. We look at privacy from neighbours and how you arrive at the house, because those decisions are difficult to undo once the layout is set.

What to bring to a first conversation

An address or a rough idea of where you want to build is enough to get started. It helps to share what you want to change, who the home is for, and any priorities around timing or budget.
